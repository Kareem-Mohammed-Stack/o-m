Who we are

O&M (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates this online store and sells apparel and related accessories. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at hello@oandm.com.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide when you create an account, place an order, join the newsletter, or contact support. This can include your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, billing address, and order notes. We also collect device and usage data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and referring URLs through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use information

We use your information to process orders, provide customer service, send shipping updates, improve the storefront, prevent fraud, and (if you opt in) send product news and offers. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

Cookies help the store remember your cart, keep you signed in, and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect checkout and account features.

Sharing

We share information with service providers who help us run the store, including Shopify, payment processors, shipping carriers, and email tools. They may only use your information to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, customers, or the public.

Payments and Shopify

This store is hosted on Shopify. Payment details are processed by Shopify and the payment providers you choose at checkout. O&M does not store full credit-card numbers on our own servers. Shopify’s privacy practices are described in Shopify’s documentation and privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, email hello@oandm.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact O&M at hello@oandm.com or through the contact page on this website.
