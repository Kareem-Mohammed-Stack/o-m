Eligibility window

You may request a return on most unworn O&M items within 30 days of delivery. The item must be in original condition, unwashed, and with tags attached. Sale items follow the same window unless the product page says otherwise.

Condition of goods

Returned products should be unused and suitable for resale. Items that show wear, odor, stains, or missing labels may be refused or refunded at a reduced amount. We recommend trying pieces on over clean clothing and keeping packaging until you are sure.

How to request a return

Email hello@oandm.com with your order number, the items you want to return, and the reason. We will send return instructions. Do not send items back without confirmation. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is caused by our error or a defective product.

Refund timing

Once we receive and inspect the return, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing usually takes 5–10 business days after we accept the return. Bank or card networks may need extra time to show the credit.

Non-returnable items

Gift cards, final-sale pieces marked as such, and items returned after the 30-day window are not eligible. Underwear, socks, and hygiene-sensitive accessories cannot be returned if opened, unless they arrived damaged or incorrect.

Exchanges

If you need a different size or color, request a return for the original item and place a new order for the replacement. We cannot guarantee restocks. For defective or incorrect items, O&M will cover return shipping and send a replacement when stock allows.
