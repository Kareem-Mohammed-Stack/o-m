Processing time

O&M packs orders on business days. Most in-stock items leave the warehouse within 1–2 business days after payment is confirmed. Preorders and made-to-order pieces follow the timeline shown on the product page.

Methods

Available shipping methods and prices are shown at checkout based on your address and order weight. We use standard tracked carriers. Expedited options may appear when they are offered for your region.

Tracking

When your order ships, we email a tracking number. Use that link for live updates. If tracking has not moved for several days after the first scan, contact us at hello@oandm.com with your order number.

International orders

We ship internationally where checkout allows an address. Delivery times are typically 7–21 business days after dispatch. Import duties, taxes, and customs delays are the customer’s responsibility and are not included in the product price unless stated at checkout.

Delays

Weather, carrier capacity, peak seasons, and customs inspections can extend delivery. We will share what we know from the carrier, but we cannot control third-party transit after handover.

Damaged packages

If a parcel arrives damaged or the contents are missing, photograph the packaging and products and email us within 48 hours of delivery. Keep all materials until the claim is resolved. We will arrange a replacement or refund according to the refund policy.
